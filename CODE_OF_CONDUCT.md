# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome constructive contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and give others room to learn.
- Respect privacy, consent and the scope of the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, impersonation, spam and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through [Alessio Bussolari's contact page](https://www.bussolarialessio.me/contact). Include only the information needed to explain the concern. Do not post personal information in public issues.

The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, restrict participation or remove access according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of the people involved; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is legally required.

This policy applies in BussoLabs repositories and their issue, pull request and discussion spaces, and when someone officially represents the community.
